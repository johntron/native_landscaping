Plant Data Conflicts Queue

Unadjudicated fields from the plant-data claim store (claims_conflicts) — either a source disagreement or a claim already flagged for review. Per 09 §4, neither disputed value shows as a fact anywhere else in this app until you resolve it here: pick a value, give a reason, and submit. Submitting only records the correction — it is replayed into the claim store on the next rebuild, so this row will keep appearing here until then.

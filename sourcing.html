    Nurseries

    NICE! partner nurseries

    NICE! (Natives Improve and Conserve Environments) is, in NPSOT’s words, “a collaboration between the Native Plant Society of Texas and local nurseries to offer natives that are right for our local environment.” The chapter supplies signs and plant information at the point of sale.

    A partner nursery is not a native-only nursery. Partnership covers the natives a nursery highlights, not everything on the lot. Look for the NICE! signs, check the botanical name on the tag, and compare it against the invasives list on Start here.
